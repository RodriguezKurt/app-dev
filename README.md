# My Favorite Anime: Dragon Ball

## About the Series
Dragon Ball is a popular Japanese anime created by Akira Toriyama. It follows the adventures of Goku, a Saiyan warrior who trains hard, becomes stronger, and protects the Earth from powerful enemies.

## Why I Like Dragon Ball
I like Dragon Ball because of its:
- Amazing fight scenes
- Powerful transformations
- Interesting characters
- Exciting tournaments
- Strong friendships and rivalries
